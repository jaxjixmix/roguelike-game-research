### Commentary


#### Entry 1

- **Part:** 2
- **Label:** Tier list Part 2 (2024)
- **Summary:** Risk of Rain Returns is the 2023 remake of the original 2D action roguelike Risk of Rain. PepperHead played 80+ hours of the original and sees this as simply the better version: same core game and art style, cleaner, with more content, characters and items. He places it in a very high A tier, saying it is only below S because he personally prefers two other games, and that it could be an easy S for many people.
- **Quote:** it's just the better risk of rain one
- **Rerank Note:** Remake of Risk of Rain 1, which he reviewed on his original tier list; this game was not out then. He notes the slow post-boss pacing issue he raised there is now fixed.

##### Praise

- Faithful remake that keeps the core of the original while improving nearly everything
- Same art style, just cleaner
- More content: characters, items, and unique challenges
- Great diversity between characters, with fun core gameplay mechanics as the focus
- Constant item pickups keep runs rewarding and pacing fast
- Removed the need to clear the whole map after the boss (toggleable in options for fans of the old way)
- New challenges offer an easier alternative way to unlock alternate skill sets

##### Criticism

- Items and abilities do not change how you play a run, usually a weak sign, though it works here
- Some may find the unlock challenges make alternate skills too easy to get, though he disagrees
- Placed below S mainly due to personal preference for other games rather than flaws

##### Unique Points

- Original was among the first action-based roguelikes, so a modern remake benefits from years of genre learning
- Options let players choose original or faster post-boss pacing, so both old and new fans are served
- Low-impact items still work because the core gameplay is the fun and loot flow is constant

- Core gameplay feel: the fun comes mostly from the core mechanics
- Character diversity: great variety between characters
- Item/ability impact: items do not change run style, yet it works

- Unlock systems: challenges make alternate skills easier to unlock
- Visuals: same art style as the original, cleaner

#### Best Things

- Faithful but much more polished remake: sharper pixel art that keeps the original's look, plus quality-of-life options that can mostly be toggled individually.
- Soundtrack and atmosphere repeatedly called out by critics and Metacritic users as a highlight.
- Large new content layer: new survivors (for example the Drifter, a scrap-gathering brawler), extra items and Risk of Rain 2 fan-favorite content alongside the classic roster (about 15 survivors on the store page).
- Item-stacking power fantasy: players like growing from weak to screen-clearing god and learning which items and survivors combine best.
- Overhauled multiplayer with up to four players online and local split-screen, which critics (GameSkinny, for one) single out as the best way to play.
- Short-ish, replayable runs and no-DLC completeness, which Steam reviewers appreciate compared with the sequel.
- Accessible to some newcomers and a clear upgrade for returning fans: several reviewers call it the right way to do a remake, while others say the original felt too hard or dated to them.
- Feels distinct from RoR2 in a positive way: a 2D, more arcade-like cousin that some players prefer for its aesthetics and pacing.
- Co-op is the emotional hook in the most-upvoted reviews: couples and parents play it with partners and kids, and the chaotic item-grabbing and frequent deaths are treated as part of the fun.

#### Worst Things

- Combat readability: late-game hordes overlap, enemy attacks have little telegraphing, and players say it is hard to tell when they have been hit.
- Flying enemies and magma worms are widely described as oppressive, since 2D aiming and movement limit how you answer them.
- Core loop feels repetitive to some: walk away from the crowd and spam skills, little positioning depth or macro play compared with RoR2.
- Providence Trials are required for some unlocks, and reviewers call the challenge design punishing rather than teaching.
- Technical rough edges in reviews: crashes, freezes when the screen fills up, sticky controller input, and controller deadzone or menu issues.
- Multiplayer connection problems reported (disconnected from host errors) and local co-op camera or scale issues on TVs.
- No mid-run save, which The Games Machine flagged, and some players lose a run after force closing.

#### Improvements

- More post-launch updates and content; 'lack of updates' is a repeated complaint even among positive reviews.
- Better feedback and telegraphing of enemy attacks and hits, and clearer screen readability in late-game chaos.
- Mid-run saving and more robust stability and performance when many objects are on screen.
- Fix multiplayer connection reliability and improve split-screen camera behavior for local co-op.
- Rework unlock gating so that Providence Trials are not needed for core item and ability unlocks, and improve controller handling and remapping.

#### Unique Things

- Difficulty scales with elapsed time rather than stage count, forcing a tradeoff between looting thoroughly and moving on.
- Huge roster of stackable items whose interactions are unpredictable, producing build variety that players call esoteric and satisfying.
- Survivors with very distinct kits plus alternate skill unlocks, and Artifacts that mutate a run.
- Providence Trials, a set of skill challenges that double as unlock content.
- A 2D side-scrolling co-op format with online and local play, unusual next to the 3D Risk of Rain 2.
- Reviewers frame it as remake-plus-superset: it keeps the original's feel while folding in RoR2 content.

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Very short, sparse item availability per map makes scavenging feel slow while enemies outscale you, per a critical review.
- Randomness of drops (especially the active item) means many runs feel alike to some players, and progression for new survivors can feel slow.
- Providence Trial unlocks and some achievement hunts are described as gatekeeping and sadistic.
- Runs can drag at around an hour, and long late-game hordes get repetitive for some.
- Heavy difficulty for newcomers: several reviewers bounced off even after hours.

#### Divisive Points

- Returns vs Risk of Rain 2: many prefer the remake's look and feel, while others say it lacks RoR2's movement and skill expression and is overshadowed by it.
- Difficulty: fans see earned power growth, critics call it unfair, sponge-like enemies and scaling.
- Art update: most love the added detail, some prefer the simpler original pixel art.
- Conservative remake: some say it changes too little, others call it the right way to do a remaster.
