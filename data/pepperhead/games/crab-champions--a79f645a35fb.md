### Community

- **One Liner:** A third-person roguelike shooter by solo developer Noisestorm (the Crab Rave musician) where you play an armed crab clearing 28 islands across four biomes, solo or in up to 4-player co-op.
- **Consensus Summary:** Crab Champions is one of the best-reviewed Early Access roguelikes on Steam: about 97.9% positive from roughly 31,000 reviews (Overwhelmingly Positive), after launching in Early Access on 1 April 2023 at around $10. Players praise the fluid movement, satisfying gunplay, build variety and a responsive solo developer, and often compare it to Risk of Rain 2 or Gunfire Reborn. Critics have treated it as a pleasant surprise rather than a joke game (Rogueliker, mein-mmo, GameStar coverage), but there is no aggregate critic score. It is still in Early Access with console versions hoped for but not confirmed in what I found. The recent negative reviews are mostly about a major update that reworked maps, difficulty and loot, plus a long Prismatic grind, not about the core game.
- **Reception Notes:** Launched in Steam Early Access on 1 April 2023 (five years after Crab Rave) and was near 98% positive from the start; it is still around 98% in 2026 with around 800 concurrent players at last check. Metacritic has a page but shows tbd for both critic and user scores (only two user ratings), and I did not find an OpenCritic entry. A major update in autumn 2025 (graphics rework, new islands, new difficulty tier above Diamond, loot changes, and a hotfix in early November) produced a wave of negative recent reviews even though overall score stayed very high. Windows only on Steam; console release and split-screen are requested by players but I found no confirmation. Earlier content updates such as the Elemental update were praised by critics like Rogueliker for not wasting players time.

#### Best Things

- Fluid, momentum-based movement (sliding, jumping, dodging sideways) that players compare to Risk of Rain 2 and Doom-style movement shooters
- Deep build-crafting with very broken, synergy-heavy combos and an enormous variety of weapons, abilities, perks and melee
- Short-to-long flexible runs (roughly 20-40 minutes, or hours if you loop the game) that are easy to jump into and easy to sink a weekend into
- Co-op for up to four players, with item and money trading and strong word-of-mouth (one reviewer gifted it to about 15 friends)
- Customisable difficulty: stackable modifiers that score points towards harder tiers for both casual and hardcore runs
- Responsive single developer who reads and filters community feedback and keeps shipping updates
- Synthwave-style soundtrack and goofy, positive crab-with-a-shotgun identity; no microtransactions, cosmetics unlocked via play
- Good optimisation and QoL such as auto-pickup of money and auto-looting the map

#### Worst Things

- Visual clutter: chaotic particle effects, low-contrast enemies and health bars are hard to read, especially in turret builds; no colour-blind options yet
- Frame-rate drops and even crashes when huge numbers of projectiles, clones or chests spawn in extreme builds
- Late-game and top-difficulty balance swings: Prismatic/Diamond feel punishing, with instant-death style mechanics, tiny boss arenas and lunging enemies, per negative reviews
- Loot RNG feels harsher after the big update, with good perks moved to higher rarities and fewer epics and legendaries seen
- Some players call the game shallow or repetitive once everything is unlocked, with similar-feeling weapons and little new content at the end
- Reports of cloud-save and unlock-progress loss for a few players, and startup crashes for others
- Enemies occasionally spawn in unreachable spots, and small or restrictive maps leave little cover
- Several post-overhaul reviews say movement feels sluggish ('stuck in mud') and that beloved fun items were nerfed in the name of balance

#### Improvements

- Colour-blind and enemy-visibility options plus less particle noise
- Ease the top difficulty grind and avoid forcing players to redo every weapon to earn the new top rank
- Restore larger, more varied islands or add a hard cap on simultaneous elites
- Better tutorial and a simpler lobby system for new players
- Permanent lobby upgrades and more late-game content or variety
- Split-screen and console versions, which reviewers request explicitly

#### Unique Things

- Made by the Irish musician behind the Crab Rave meme, which began as an April Fools joke and grew into a real, polished game
- Crab-themed third-person shooter with platforming-style acrobatics, a rare mix in a genre dominated by top-down and first-person games
- Looping runs that can go past 100 rounds, with stacking greed perks that scale enemy spawns into absurd numbers
- Extremely modular difficulty system where player-chosen modifiers define the challenge tier
- Turret and ability-mod builds, elemental effects (ice, etc.) and melee claws alongside a fully automatic rifle loadout

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Grinding Prismatic: clearing the game twice (56 islands) and redoing every weapon for the new top tier
- Collecting all-items and diamond achievements, described as not a fun process by long-time players
- Gauntlet portals that reviewers say are too hard for too little reward
- Extreme chest or spawn scaling that slows or crashes the game
- Repeated islands with similar layouts after many hours, per some reviewers and Reddit-style discussions
- Heavy RNG dependence when chasing 100% completion across every weapon, called painful by players who finished it before the overhaul

#### Divisive Points

- The late-2025 overhaul (new graphics, revamped islands, reworked difficulty and loot): some beta players loved the optimisation and quality of life changes, while others say maps are smaller and less fun and loot is nerfed
- Developer balance direction: some say the dev caters to hardcore top-percent players and nerfs fun builds, while others praise the dev for separating good feedback from bad
- Whether the game is deep or shallow: fans call the build variety enormous, a minority say the core loop is thin
