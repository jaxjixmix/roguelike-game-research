##### Praise

- Cute hand-drawn paper visuals with a Flash-game vibe
- Simple, straightforward core gameplay: move, teleport, aim with cursor, dodge yellow bullets, block red ones
- Roguelike items create chaos that looks different every run
- Bosses have interesting mechanics and require attention and care
- Different planets play drastically differently (moving planet, spaceship orbiting), giving the best replayability

##### Criticism

- Runs feel repetitive: same biomes and same few bosses every time
- Regular enemies are one-shot so only bosses pose a threat
- Being overpowered almost every run makes it feel less rewarding and less unique
- Meta progression is weak: a shop with random unknown items (others vanish when you pick one) and minimal, situational upgrades
- Wants more bosses and more in-run content; unsure he will return to it

##### Unique Points

- The game shows an error message when too many bullets fill the screen and need to be removed
- Unlockable planets change the playstyle radically, such as a moving planet or a spaceship not on the planet
- Simple extra block mechanic for red bullets used sparingly

#### Best Things

- Hand-drawn, notebook-doodle, Flash-game art style that is coherent and charming
- Item synergies are wacky and build-defining (bee-bullet swarms, electric walls, teleporting rocket bombs, bullet recycling)
- 1-4 player local couch co-op with Remote Play Together, good for chaotic party play
- Lots of unlockable planets/characters, modes, modifiers and visuals tied to achievements
- Option to skip the metaprogression grind and unlock everything immediately (praised by a 21-hour reviewer)
- Fast, pick-up-and-play arcade runs with enough variety to be 'highly addictive' for a few hours

#### Worst Things

- Balance is lopsided: runs depend on getting a broken build, and without one boss fights become minutes of low-damage waiting
- Most stats feel nearly meaningless; bullet count, fire rate and bullet recycling dominate while raw damage does little
- At least 8 achievements reported broken for over 2 years, and '100% is bugged' is repeated in multiple reviews
- Heavily luck-based, leaving little room for player skill according to some reviewers
- Item upgrades lack descriptions, so players can't tell what they are picking (one review and a Steam discussion about item descriptions not showing)
- Content runs out fast for some, with the loop feeling repetitive after an hour or two
- Performance issues such as FPS drops in boss fights and lag from heavy bullet counts
- Seen as too pricey for its content at $14.99 by at least one reviewer, even on discount

#### Improvements

- Fix the long-broken achievements so 100% completion is possible
- Rebalance damage vs. fire rate/bullet count so that stats matter and weak runs are not a slog
- Show clear item and upgrade descriptions in-game
- Fix boss-fight FPS drops and raise the damage-counter integer cap (requested in Steam threads)
- Add more content and variety to extend replay beyond a few hours
- Visible developer communication and updates, since players ask whether the game is abandoned

#### Unique Things

- You defend a fixed circular planet that cannot move, described by one reviewer as Asteroids without movement
- Blend of arcade bullet-hell shooter and tower-defense style planet defense
- Flash/doodle aesthetic with a school-notebook vibe, cartoony and family friendly
- Items combine freely with each other, leading to absurd overpowered runs
- Local co-op for up to four players on one screen
- A switch to unlock all metaprogression immediately, unusual for the genre

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Boss fights that take minutes when your build is weak and you deal little damage
- Waves of high-health enemies that become boring without good items
- Achievement hunting blocked by bugs
- Repetitive feeling after the first hour or two for some players
- Chaotic screen with no item descriptions, making it hard to read what is happening

#### Divisive Points

- Randomness: some love wild, chaotic build luck; others find it too luck-driven with too little skill
- Value: some call it a great casual game, others say it is too expensive and too short for the price
- Chaos level: fun mayhem for some, unreadable for others who bounced in under an hour
