### Community

#### Best Things

- Buttery-smooth movement and controls; you start with a dash that has i-frames plus a double jump, which players say makes platforming combat feel great
- Each of the playable characters (eight per the store page) plays noticeably differently, with easy and hard characters
- Large item pool (300+ weapons, pets and trinkets per the store page) with risk/reward unlocks
- Difficulty is customisable and deep; a reviewer with dozens of hours had only cleared the second of five difficulties for most characters
- Goofy, charming space-potato humour and hand-drawn art that contrast with grimdark roguelites
- Strong replayability and achievement hunting (100+ achievements; some players have 70-190 hours)
- Varied levels built from hundreds of hand-crafted rooms, with 50+ enemies and 10 bosses
- Short-session charm: many reviewers with 70-190 hours call it a favourite and a hidden gem, with a few rating it as high as 9/10

#### Worst Things

- Progression is slow: you level up and collect items so gradually that runs can feel like a slog before any build comes together
- Items feel sparse early in a run, so the game leans heavily on the core combat being fun, which some players find merely okay
- Some players found the movement and combat unsatisfying or unhappy-making (short negative reviews)
- Steam community describes it as light on content overall
- Reported technical problems in the forums: the game not launching for some and progress not saving on exit
- The mobile version reportedly does not feel good to play compared with PC or console

#### Improvements

- Rebalance or speed up levelling and item acquisition so runs reach their payoff sooner (most commonly requested in recent reviews)
- A developer revisit or content update; several recent reviewers explicitly ask Blobfish to update the game
- Mod support to extend content, requested in the Steam community
- More content depth, since some players say it runs out of things to do
- Clearer explanation of how to unlock higher difficulty (danger) modes
- Fix launch and save-progress bugs reported in discussions

#### Unique Things

- Plays as a potato-style prisoner gladiator in a space prison, with deliberately absurd cast and tone
- Mix of arena-style combat and platforming with dash i-frames and double jump, closer to Nuclear Throne or Hollow Knight than top-down arena shooters
- Risk/reward item unlocks and a five-tier difficulty system with extra difficulty customisation
- Comes from the same studio as Brotato, so players frequently frame it as the less famous sibling with a very different (platformer) format
- Room-based levels built procedurally from many hand-crafted rooms, plus treasure rooms and merchants

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Slow levelling that makes the mid-run feel grindy and not worth the time, even for players who enjoy the game
- Long runs before item impact is felt, described as boring by a Brotato fan who stopped after minutes
- Hard characters and upper difficulty tiers can be punishing, even on the easy characters according to one reviewer
- Unlock grind for items and achievements is a long haul, though some like it
- Save and launch issues mentioned by community posters

#### Divisive Points

- Pace of runs: fans call it addictive and balanced, while others find it a slow slog
- Comparison with Brotato: some see it as underrated, others say it is only decent next to that hit
- Difficulty: praised as deep and challenging by veterans, but a barrier for players who want faster payoff
- Core combat feel: described as smooth and satisfying by most, unsatisfying by a few
