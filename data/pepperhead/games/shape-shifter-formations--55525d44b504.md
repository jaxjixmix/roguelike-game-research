- **Summary:** A neon-styled survivor-like where you pilot a small ship with a single aimed weapon, a dash and a ship-specific ultimate. He is blown away by the amount of content, build variety and level-up choice depth, calling it one of his favorite survivor-likes and a hidden gem. - **Rerank Note:**

##### Praise

- Absurd amount of content: roughly 20 ships, about 15 weapons, many unlocks via achievements
- Level-up screens offer four choices that can be banished, skipped, rerolled, duplicated, plus a glitch system that can double an upgrade
- Upgrades are mostly mechanical rather than number tweaks, with builds that synergize across corrosion, lightning, fire and more
- Multiple layered progression trees (skill tree, paragon, ultimate tree) plus rare legendary upgrades with big impact
- Shop fills with new mechanics as you unlock, giving lots of player choice
- Chaotic, satisfying neon visuals and tight control with close dodges feeling great
- Has co-op/multiplayer and strong solo play

##### Criticism

- Takes a while to get going since you must unlock a lot to have real build potential
- Number-adjustment trees (skill tree/paragon) unlock late; he wishes they came earlier
- No luck stat, which he feels the game could benefit from
- Some bosses are far harder than others
- Some bosses have no adds, which undercuts on-kill and bounce synergies
- Some balance issues here and there

##### Unique Points

- Single-weapon, dash and ultimate core loop differs from typical multi-weapon survivor-likes
- Level-up manipulation tools (banish, reroll, duplicate, tag prioritizing/removal) are something he has not seen elsewhere
- Legendary artifact unlocks found roughly once every few runs that alter on-kill/on-hit behavior

##### Comparisons

- Nova Drift

- Synergies: builds intertwine across damage types and mechanics
- Build diversity: many ships, weapons and upgrade paths
- Player choice: heavy level-up and shop options with manipulation tools
- Meta-progression: multiple skill trees, achievements, unlocks, though slow start
- Visuals/charm: neon art style and chaotic spectacle
- Boss/enemy design: uneven difficulty and bosses lacking adds
- Balance: some tuning issues

#### Best Things

- Deep build variety: 20 ships, 20 weapons, about 800 skills in skill trees, and 200+ infinitely stacking upgrades make runs feel different
- Level-up choice tools (reroll, banish, lock, transmute, dual pick, plus a tag system to promote or blacklist upgrade tags) give real control over builds, which reviewers say keeps late-run picks exciting
- Strong Geometry Wars feel with clean, readable neon visuals; reviewers say they rarely got lost even in hectic moments
- Satisfying meta-progression and unlock loop that gives a 'one more run' hook and starts paying off once you unlock more of the skill tree
- Difficulty is seen as fair, with scaling threat levels and deaths that usually feel like build or skill problems rather than pure RNG
- Good local co-op and ongoing developer support (1.0 added a final boss arena, three save profiles, and several balance/bug patches)
- Flexible settings: optional auto-aim, toggle auto-fire added in 1.0.1 for accessibility, and a granular difficulty-modifier system
- Runs are around 30 minutes with an endless mode, so they are easy to fit into short sessions

#### Worst Things

- Many unlocks do not say how to get them; a reviewer could not work out how to unlock the Swarm ship because locked items do not name what unlocks them
- No wiki or official Discord was noted by one negative reviewer at 1.0, which makes the opaque unlock system worse
- Performance: a Steam Deck reviewer measured FPS dropping to 1-2 for seconds at threat level 25 and occasional full freezes; another positive reviewer had to change video settings to get normal performance
- Save-data loss reported by at least one reviewer (51 hours lost), and the Steam forum has a pinned 1.0 saves notice and a bug about restarts forgetting tags and bans
- Startup problems reported on the forum: hangs on first launch and prompts to install the .NET Framework
- Some upgrade text is unclear or misleading (for example, how overkill works, and '100% reduction' not reducing a duration to zero)
- Balance gaps: the stealth ship is called overpowered and long-range/auto-target weapons much easier than others; one forum thread calls Ability Duration Up bad

#### Improvements

- Add online co-op; reviewers note the game only has local co-op and the forum has a complaint about needing a controller in multiplayer
- Explain unlock conditions and upgrade mechanics better in-game (name the item an unlock requires, tooltips for overkill, dual pick and percentage stacking), or provide a wiki
- Fix performance spikes at high threat levels and Steam Deck stutters
- Rebalance ships and weapons so non-stealth ships and manual-aim weapons compete, and buff weak upgrades like Ability Duration Up
- Improve save reliability and preserve tags and bans when restarting a run
- Add more content after 1.0 (reviewers explicitly hope for more)

#### Unique Things

- Tag system that lets you promote or blacklist upgrade categories in the level-up pool, which one reviewer credits with dramatically improving the roguelike element versus similar games
- Unusual level-up options: reroll, banish, lock, transmute and dual select in the same run
- Geometry Wars twin-stick action fused with a survivor-like/bullet-heaven upgrade structure and infinite stacking upgrades
- Ship-specific and weapon-specific skill trees with around 800 skills, giving much deeper pre-run customization than typical bullet-heavens
- Threat-level (heat/NG+) scaling and endless looping with rising enemy counts
- Community-made UEVR mod lets you play it in VR, which a reviewer raves about

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Slow start: one reviewer says it takes a while to get going and feels weak until you unlock skill-tree nodes
- Unlock grind: a player at 12 hours had unlocked only about 25% of content and found the hidden unlock conditions annoying
- Chaotic late-game screens for players using non-auto-target weapons, where dodging and max damage dominate
- Performance stalls mid-run that cannot be played around because the ship is immobile or the run ends

#### Divisive Points

- Grind: one reviewer calls it one of the best 'mindless grind' roguelikes while another finds the unlock system frustratingly opaque
- Balance of ease versus challenge: some reviewers say difficulty is well tuned with a good threat spike, while others find non-stealth and manual-aim ships much harder than auto-target builds
- Local-only co-op is praised as great co-op by some and a dealbreaker limitation by others
