### Community

- **One Liner:** A twin-stick, room-by-room dungeon crawler by Edmund McMillen where Isaac descends through randomly generated floors collecting hundreds of synergistic items, wrapped in grotesque religious-horror imagery.
- **Consensus Summary:** Regarded as one of the defining roguelikes: Steam shows 96.8% positive across about 459k reviews (Overwhelmingly Positive) and critics landed in the high 80s for Rebirth (Metacritic 88 on 15 reviews, OpenCritic 87 with 92% recommending). Players praise near-endless replayability from 700+ items and 34 characters, tight shooting, and the bleak-funny identity. The recurring complaints are RNG swings, opaque item text, a long unlock and completion grind, and steep difficulty for newcomers. The game is fully released and very much alive: Repentance is the final expansion and the community keeps it going with mods and Workshop content, and a 2026 patch triggered some DLC-loading complaints on Steam.
- **Reception Notes:** The 2011 Flash original sold about 700k copies in under a year and passed 2 million by late 2012 (per news reports), which funded the Rebirth remake by McMillen and Nicalis (Nov 2014, new engine, co-op, hundreds of new items). Afterbirth, Afterbirth+ and the 2021 Repentance expansion followed (OpenCritic 88, 82 and 83 per search results). Steam-revenue-calculator estimates for Rebirth are about $225M gross and about $66M net (Boxleiter-style, order-of-magnitude only); SteamSpy lists 5 to 10 million owners. Metacritic page re-fetched: Metascore 88 on 15 reviews, user 8.2 on 1,154 ratings. Current Steam reviews (2026) are still overwhelmingly positive, with many ironic 'I hate this game' recommendations from 1000+ hour players, a few complaints about a recent patch breaking DLC loading, and multiplayer desync complaints.

#### Best Things

- Huge replayability: 700+ items, 188 trinkets, 34 characters (with Tainted versions) and thousands of hours are cited by long-time players
- Item synergies produce wildly different builds each run (Brimstone, Tech X and similar combos are commonly named as highlights)
- Tight, responsive twin-stick combat with a fast, addictive 'one more run' loop
- Strong, committed dark and gross aesthetic with a memorable soundtrack and a story told through endings and secrets
- Repentance rebalanced overpowered and weak items, added new routes, bosses and thousands of rooms; one review gave the DLC 9.5/10
- Very good value, especially on sale, given the content volume; Steam Workshop mods extend it further
- Hidden secrets and discoverable mechanics reward curiosity and community knowledge-sharing

#### Worst Things

- Item effects are poorly explained in-game, so many players rely on mods or the wiki to know what pickups do
- Heavy RNG: item pools are bloated, so bad rolls and unwinnable seeds are common, and top players restart early floors until they find a strong first item
- Hostile onboarding: new players report difficulty even learning controls and the game's systems, with little guidance
- Some content gates are harsh: powerful items like Godhead and Death Certificate require beating hard late bosses, sometimes with one-hit characters like The Lost
- Repentance-era balance: trap rooms and sudden damage can ruin runs regardless of knowledge; Delirium is described as broken by teleporting onto the player
- Multiplayer/co-op reportedly desyncs, and you cannot rejoin mid-floor
- Darker themes (child abuse, religious extremism) and crude humor put some players off; content is not for younger players
- Recent patches reportedly broke DLC loading or launch (Afterbirth+/Repentance), with some players rolling back to older versions and unable to refund

#### Improvements

- Better in-game item descriptions or an optional info display (the most popular mod simply tells you what items do)
- A gentler tutorial/onboarding so new players learn controls and core systems
- Less RNG dependence and tighter item pool curation so runs feel less dependent on early luck
- Fewer time-gated and grindy completion requirements (daily challenges, repeated hard-mode clears)
- More reliable online co-op and better patch QA so DLC does not break after updates

#### Unique Things

- Extremely dense item-interaction system with 700+ items, giving emergent builds no other roguelike matches in raw variety
- Shoot-in-four-directions dungeon crawl structure with Zelda-style rooms instead of continuous levels
- Uncompromising religious-horror and body-horror theme paired with cartoon visuals and dark comedy
- Completion meta with 34 characters, dozens of endings and a 'Dead God' 100% achievement that can take 500+ hours
- Shaped the modern roguelite genre; the series originated from a Flash game in 2011 and was remade in Rebirth in 2014

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- 100% completion and unlocks are a long grind, cited as 500+ hours, including killing final bosses repeatedly across many characters
- Daily challenges act as real-calendar time gates for certain unlocks, which players compare to mobile-game design
- Restarting early floors or rerolling until the item pool looks promising, and slower clears when pools are diluted (a reported habit, not a top review theme)
- Hard-mode requirements for best unlocks feel punishing for casual players
- Some players dislike specific characters or modes (Jacob and Esau, Curse of the Blind, Lazarus, Tainted Lost are named in reviews)
- Four-way shooting and enemy design feel restrictive or boring to a minority, with hitbox or movement complaints in a few reviews
- Forced modes for unlocks, e.g. Greed Mode, are said to stall enjoyment of the core game

#### Divisive Points

- RNG versus skill: critics call it luck-driven, while defenders point to long win streaks by top players as proof skill dominates
- Repentance: many see a masterpiece of content and balance, others feel it added chores and pushed difficulty toward hardcore players
- Low-information design: some love discovering item effects by experiment, others find it poor design
- Art style and tone: praised as distinctive and funny by fans, dismissed as teenage-edgy or off-putting by others
