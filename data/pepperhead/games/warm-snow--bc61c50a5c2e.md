##### Praise

- Takes itself seriously without turning goofy, and the tone works
- Makes you feel really powerful, with absurd scaling such as +300% damage passives
- Overpowered enemies keep the power fantasy from getting boring across runs
- Strong pre-run customization: every run starts with a boon from a different god that sets your elemental damage
- A starting relic that synergizes with the boon gives character-like choice from the start
- Visually impressive, with awesome-looking bosses

##### Criticism

- Bosses are great but always the same ones every run
- Balance is merely okay and not the best design
- Constant overpowered state could bore players in some games, though here it mostly holds up

##### Unique Points

- Everything is overpowered, enemies included, so scaling is mutually absurd rather than one-sided
- Starting god boon acts like choosing a different character by fixing the elemental damage type
- Design lesson: roguelikes benefit from varied bosses, especially action-based ones

##### Design Criteria

- Power fantasy / scaling: feeling overpowered must stay fun across many runs
- Balance: acceptable but not great given extreme numbers
- Pre-run customization: unique god boon and synergistic relic at the start
- Boss variety: same bosses every run is a disappointment
- Visuals / spectacle: visually crazy and impressive
- Tone: serious presentation that avoids being goofy

#### Best Things

- Large build variety: sects, relics, weapons and deities combine into many synergies, and players report late-game runs feel very flexible
- Distinctive gear system: the same relic or piece of gear has a different effect depending on which slot it is placed in, and can be re-slotted at any time
- Fast, fluid combat built around melee plus flying swords that can be launched and recalled
- Striking art direction inspired by ancient silk/ink painting, with Chinese mythology theme and a well-liked soundtrack
- Scalable difficulty and challenge options give high replay value; reviewers cite 30-40+ hours to unlock everything and multiple stories/endings
- Good boss fights and decent enemy variety according to several positive reviewers
- Lots of content for the price (around $18), including a free DLC and a paid expansion

#### Worst Things

- Translation problems: English text overflows UI boxes, some keywords and item effects are mistranslated or unclear, and some end-of-boss text is untranslated
- Meta-progression is described as very slow to upgrade, which a top-voted negative review calls the game's major flaw
- Fixed, linear level layouts and repeated bosses make runs feel repetitive compared with procedurally generated roguelites
- Many item and relic effects reduce to 'do more damage when X', so playstyle changes less than the item count implies
- Enemies become fodder with a decent build but feel like HP sponges in early runs or on weak builds
- Dash and movement are called clunky or slow by several reviewers; game feels dated to some
- Story and characters are seen as weak or forgettable compared with Hades, with average presentation of the narrative
- Some players report stuttering and performance problems on PC
- Weapons lean on specific relics to reach full power, and many weapons reuse the same swing animation so they feel like stat swaps

#### Improvements

- Clearer UI, status and keyword explanations and a proper retranslation of the English text
- Faster or less grindy meta-progression and unlock pacing
- More varied or procedural level layouts and more enemy types
- Better balance so that some relics/weapons are not dependent on specific relic pairings and some builds are not broken
- Stronger narrative delivery and more developed characters
- Tighter dash and movement feel

#### Unique Things

- Slot-dependent relic effects: the same item behaves differently in each of the four slots and can be rearranged freely
- Floating flying-sword projectiles alongside melee, with a recall mechanic
- Sect and deity choices at run start that shape the build, inside a Chinese mythology / dark wuxia setting
- Multiple stories (about three) with a hidden true ending that requires collecting fragments across many runs
- Extremely nuanced status-effect and condition-trigger item system (poison, bleed, burn, frost) with deep stacking rules

#### Tedious Or Disliked

- Slow meta-progression and farming enemies for fragments to unlock all stories and the true ending
- Early runs feel repetitive before enough content is unlocked
- Nothing is explained well; players often have to play to learn what a keyword does
- Fixed maps and repeated bosses get samey over a long playthrough
- Completion is a long grind: unlocking everything means farming fragments across many runs, and some reviewers regret the hours sunk into 'unlock it all'

#### Divisive Points

- Hades comparison: some praise it as a top-tier roguelite, others say Hades and its sequel exist so just play those
- Build depth: many praise huge variety, while critics say effects mostly boil down to more damage and do not change playstyle
- Difficulty and balance: some find it hard, others find bosses and enemies trivial with a good build
- Chinese voice acting and translation: some see it as flavourful, others as a barrier for English speakers
